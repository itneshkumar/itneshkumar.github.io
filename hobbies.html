Chess

Active on Chess.com and Lichess. Chess taught me that the best move is rarely obvious, that planning several steps ahead matters, and that overconfidence loses games. I see strong parallels with designing multi-agent systems where each agent needs to reason about other agents' future states.

Trekking

The Himalayas are an hour from Delhi and among the most humbling places on Earth. I try to get into the mountains whenever I can — Kedarkantha, Triund, Chopta. Trekking resets my problem-solving mode; the best ideas for system architecture often arrive at altitude.

Kedarkantha · Triund · Chopta

Food Exploration

Delhi has an embarrassingly good street food culture and I have made it my personal responsibility to document it empirically. Chandni Chowk parathas, Nizamuddin kebabs, Old Delhi chaat — each neighbourhood is a completely different cuisine. Still collecting data.
