Acoustic Guitar

Music is my ultimate creative outlet. Beyond just learning chords, I’m fascinated by the mathematical structure of music theory and the physical mechanics of the fretboard. To me, mastering a difficult solo is very similar to optimizing a neural network—it’s about repetition, refining the flow, and identifying the patterns that create the most impact.

Acoustic · Pattern Recognition · Theory

Badminton

Badminton is my go-to for high-intensity agility and focus. It’s a game of rapid decision-making under pressure—constantly calculating shuttle trajectories and opponent positioning in milliseconds. It provides the perfect physical balance to the long hours of deep work required for architecting AI systems.

Agility & Precision

Carrom

A game that rewards geometric intuition and delicate control. I enjoy the physics involved in bank shots and rebounds—the "unseen lines" on the board. Much like debugging a complex RAG pipeline, Carrom is about understanding the environment and finding the most efficient angle to reach the goal.

Tactical Geometry
